Explore Chinese Cuisine — 8 Regional Traditions

China's culinary heritage spans eight major regional cuisines, each shaped by geography, climate, and thousands of years of history. From Sichuan's fiery mala to Cantonese dim sum, from Shandong's imperial banquet dishes to Jiangsu's delicate knife-work artistry — every province tells a story through its food.

Cuisine Regions Covered

Sichuan (numbing-spicy), Guangdong/Cantonese (fresh & steamed), Shandong (Lu cuisine, imperial kitchen), Jiangsu (Huaiyang, knife skills), Zhejiang (fresh seafood, bamboo shoots), Fujian (Buddha Jumps Over the Wall), Hunan (dry-hot chili), and Anhui (wild herbs, slow-braising). Plus Beijing, Shanghai, Xinjiang, Yunnan, and Northeast Dongbei specialties.

What You'll Find

Authentic recipes with ingredient lists and step-by-step instructions, regional food culture and history essays, dining guides for major cities, and seasonal eating traditions. Every entry is researched and verified against authoritative Chinese culinary texts.
