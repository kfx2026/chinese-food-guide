Authentic Recipes

Step-by-step methods from traditional kitchens — so you can taste these dishes no matter where you are.

China's culinary heritage spans eight major regional cuisines — Sichuan's fiery mala, Cantonese dim sum, Shandong's imperial banquet dishes, Jiangsu's delicate knife-work, Zhejiang's fresh seafood, Fujian's legendary soups, Hunan's dry-hot chili, and Anhui's slow-braised wild herbs. Every province tells a story through its food. We document authentic recipes with step-by-step instructions, regional food culture and history essays, dining guides for major cities, and seasonal eating traditions.
