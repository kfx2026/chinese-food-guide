About Great Chinese Food

Exploring authentic Chinese cuisine, one province at a time.

Who We Are

Great Chinese Food is an independent publication dedicated to documenting and celebrating the diverse culinary traditions of China. Our team researches and curates information about regional dishes, cooking techniques, and food culture from all 34 provinces and regions of China.

Our Mission

We believe that food is the most accessible gateway to understanding Chinese culture. Every province has unique flavors shaped by geography, history, and local ingredients. Our mission is to make this rich culinary heritage discoverable to food lovers worldwide.

Editorial Standards

All content is researched and written by our editorial team. We source information from culinary historians, regional cookbooks, food documentary archives, and direct interviews with local chefs and home cooks. Each province guide is reviewed for accuracy before publication.
